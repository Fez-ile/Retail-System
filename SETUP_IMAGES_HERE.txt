## Image Recommendations for Your Brand

Since AMMS is a luxury brand, follow these guidelines:

### Photography Style
- **Clean and minimal** - White or neutral backgrounds
- **Professional quality** - High resolution, sharp focus
- **Consistent editing** - All images should have similar tone/lighting
- **Editorial feel** - Magazine-style photography
- **Black, white, beige, grey tones** - Match brand colors
- **Elegant composition** - Use rule of thirds in framing

### Free Stock Photo Resources
- **Unsplash.com** - High-quality free images
- **Pexels.com** - Free stock photos
- **Pixabay.com** - Free images
- Search: "fashion", "luxury", "minimalist", "elegant", "clothing"

### DIY Options
- Take your own product photography
- Use Canva.com to design graphics
- Edit with free tools like Photoshop Express

## Troubleshooting

### Images Not Showing?
1. Check filenames are EXACT (lowercase, with hyphens)
2. Verify files are in `assets/images/` folder
3. Clear browser cache: `Ctrl+Shift+Delete` then refresh
4. Check file permissions (should be readable)

### Images Look Blurry?
1. Use higher resolution source image
2. Don't resize larger than original
3. Use JPG compression carefully

### Page Loading Slow?
1. Compress images more aggressively
2. Reduce dimensions if needed
3. Consider using JPG instead of PNG
